Getting started

What is Soom?

Soom is a personal protocol app. It brings your micros, sessions, calendar events and body data together on one timeline, so you can see how your protocol fits around the rest of your day.

Soom does not create or prescribe health protocols. You decide what belongs in yours.

What is a micro?

A micro is a small action within your protocol: take creatine, morning sunlight, a glass of water, screens off. Micros can be scheduled for a time of day and repeated on the days you choose.

What is a session?

A session is a block of time reserved for something in your protocol: training, a walk, sauna, meditation. Sessions sit on the timeline next to your micros and calendar events.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login and no Soom account. Everything lives on your phone.

Which devices does Soom run on?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. There is no iPad, Mac, Android or web version yet. They are on our list, and we will say so here when one is ready.

Apple Health

What can Soom show?

Depending on what your devices write to Apple Health: sleep, deep sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps and time in daylight. Soom only reads these. It never writes to Health.

Does Soom work with my watch or ring?

If your device writes supported data to Apple Health, Soom can use it once you grant permission. What shows up depends on the device, its app, and which Health data types it supports. We are planning to publish a list of apps and devices we have tested, so you can check yours before you start.

How do I change what Soom can read?

Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Soom

You can turn individual data types on or off at any time. Soom works without any of them; the stats simply stay empty.

Calendar

Calendar sync is part of Soom Pro. Once it's on, your iOS Calendar events appear on the Soom timeline alongside your protocol. Soom only reads events; it never creates or edits them.

My calendar events are not appearing

Three things to check. First, that you have Soom Pro; calendar sync is part of it. Second, that iOS allows Soom to read your calendars:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Soom

Third, that the Calendar switch in Soom's own Settings is on. It turns off by itself if Pro lapses, and comes back on when you renew.

Widgets

Soom widgets show what's coming up in your day on your Home Screen. If a widget stops updating:

  1. Open Soom, so it can refresh.
  2. Check that Health and Calendar permissions are still granted.
  3. Remove the widget and add it again.
  4. Restart your iPhone if it still lags behind.

No streaks, scores or penalties

Soom is deliberately different from habit trackers. There are no streaks to protect and no score for completing your day. Your protocol should be able to change with your life, so you can complete, move, skip, add or remove any part of it whenever you need to. Soom will not punish you for it.

Soom Pro

What Pro includes

Two things: your iOS Calendar on the timeline, and creating backups of your whole protocol. Everything else in Soom is free, and restoring from a backup is always free.

Trial and renewal

Pro comes as a monthly or yearly subscription, billed through your Apple account, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers. After the trial it renews automatically unless you cancel before the current period ends. The exact price shows in the app in your local currency before you confirm anything.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Soom.

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Soom Pro

You can switch between monthly and yearly there, or cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all refunds and we cannot issue them ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple account you bought with.

I have Pro but the app says Free

Make sure the phone is signed in to the App Store with the Apple account you subscribed with. Then open Soom Settings, tap Activate Pro, and use Restore purchases. If it still shows Free, email us with the date you subscribed.

Backups and restore

Where does a backup go?

A backup is one file, saved wherever you choose: iCloud Drive, the Files app, or anywhere iOS can save to. Soom does not keep a copy. If you lose the file, we cannot recover it.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone, create a backup and save it to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Soom, open Settings, tap Restore from backup, and pick the file. Restoring is free and does not need Pro.

Restore replaces what's on the phone

Restoring a backup replaces the protocol currently in the app. If you've made changes you want to keep, create a fresh backup first.

Privacy

What stays on your phone

Your micros, sessions, notes, calendar events and everything read from Apple Health. None of it is uploaded anywhere. There is no Soom server holding your protocol.

What leaves your phone

Three things, none of them tied to your name: anonymous usage events so we know which screens get used, crash reports so we can fix bugs, and your purchase status so Pro works. Health and calendar data are never part of any of it. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, that removes everything. If you'd also like us to delete the anonymous analytics from your install, email us and we will.

Troubleshooting

If Soom isn't behaving as expected, these fix most things:

  • Update Soom to the latest version
  • Update iOS
  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your iPhone
  • Check Health and Calendar permissions
  • Restore purchases if Pro isn't recognised

If the problem continues

Email hi@soomprotocol.com and include:

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or a short screen recording, if relevant
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your Soom version and build, shown at the bottom of Soom Settings, for example 1.0.0 (18)

Please don't include health details unless they're needed to explain the problem.
